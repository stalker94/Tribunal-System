# Tribunal-System

Depending on the type of Policy sustained by a Faction; the Trials and Tribunals System will be adapted in one way or another. Thus, a Faction under Dictatorship will not act the same as a Faction under Democracy.  --The system said will be based on making decisions about individuals who have generated criminal problems, so that the Factions will have their Laws, their Prisons and the Punishments to impose according to the crime committed. --If this System is not automated and it is the player who is responsible for coordinating it, all kinds of decisions can be made such as declaring the individual innocent, sometimes risking serious repercussions among the population of your Faction or acting with a stern hand calling an execution to prove to your own what will not be allowed among yours.
